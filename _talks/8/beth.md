---
title: "Cap & Buy Back: How Rent Control Could Pay for Social Housing"
speaker: "Beth Stratford"
evening: 8
emoji: 🧢
hook: "Every economist knows where rent control leads: Stockholm, with its decade-long queues to get a home."
---

Beth begs to differ. New HMRC data shows what landlords really earn, and what taxpayers pay them in housing benefit: billions a year that could go to social housing instead. Her case: cap the rents, let landlords sell, and buy their homes for social rent with the savings.
